Spring Cleaning: Decluttering Tips for Oklahoma Homeowners

Oklahoma City Junk Removal

Spring in Oklahoma comes with its own decluttering priorities — storm season is on the way, temperatures swing fast, and a winter's worth of clutter has usually piled up in the garage. Here's where to focus.

Check storm-related storage first

Before storm season ramps up, it's worth clearing out anything blocking access to storm shelters, safe rooms, or emergency supplies. Garages and basements tend to accumulate boxes and equipment over winter that can get in the way when you need fast access.

Sort through winter gear you won't use again

Space heaters, extra blankets, and cold-weather equipment that didn't get used, or that you're replacing, are easy candidates to clear out in spring rather than storing for another year.

Get ahead of yard waste

Spring storms often mean downed branches and yard debris on top of normal seasonal cleanup. Rather than letting yard waste pile up over several weekends, it's usually easier to deal with it in one go once the worst of it has accumulated.

Use the season change as a decision point

Spring cleaning works well as a forcing function: if something has been sitting untouched since last spring, it's a good sign it's not getting used. Go room by room and apply that one-year test rather than trying to make case-by-case decisions on everything at once.

Don't let it pile up in the garage

The most common outcome of spring cleaning is a garage full of "get rid of" piles that sit for months. Once you've sorted, get it hauled away in one trip rather than letting it become next year's clutter problem.
